## Delivery tips

- **Smile on the first line.** Pause for one beat after "this is Credence".
- **State the problem with conviction.** Slow down on "DISCONNECTED from their payments".
- **Speak to Credence the way a shopkeeper would:** natural Hindi, normal speed. Don't over-enunciate.
- **Let the phone notification breathe:** hold about 1.5 s on screen before "And there it is".
- **Close slower than you opened.** The last line should land: "credit, / built on trust."
